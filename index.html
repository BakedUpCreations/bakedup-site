About Baked Up

Baked Up, LLC was established in early 2018 in Austin, Texas. We focus on bringing people together to enjoy good times with new and old friends. Treating everyone with kindness and working together are two principles we truly take to heart.

We strive to always do the right thing, be good to people, help each other out, and ensure people from all walks of life feel welcomed — and most of all, to have a crazy amount of fun with one another. We share our creativity by throwing events and livestreams, and by offering products inspired by our surroundings and the amazing people we've met along the way.

However you choose to enjoy Baked Up, our Sugaa Squaad can't wait to make memories with you, your friends and your family that you'll want to relive.
